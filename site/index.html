08 / FAQ

Questions.

The short version. The full rules and the command reference live in the docs.

Is any of this real money?

No. aUSD is a number in your browser. You can't deposit it, withdraw it or buy it. There is no login, no top-up and no wallet to connect.

Will there be real money?

Real-money trading on Robinhood Chain is coming in the future. There is no date yet and nothing to deposit today.

Where do the prices come from?

Crypto and macro: Hyperliquid's public API (mark, oracle, hourly funding, open interest, order book, trades). Stock tokens: their pools on Robinhood Chain, read through a public RPC every three seconds, with candles from GeckoTerminal. Your browser connects to all of it directly.

What happens while I'm away?

When you come back, Aven fetches the candles and funding history you missed and replays them in order, so stops, fills, funding and liquidations land when they would have.

Is my account saved?

In this browser only. Clearing site data or switching devices starts you over. The journal can export your trades as CSV.
